## Hotel decision and commercial activation now added

- Hotel selection is not a restaurant-style list and not one card per portal.
  `booking.stay-decision.v1` deduplicates the same property, rejects incomplete
  or stale bait prices and compares providers only for equivalent stay/rate
  products with complete mandatory charges.
- The compact user result has three explainable lanes: cheapest comparable
  total, best flexible option and best personal fit. Affiliate commission is
  excluded from every result-ranking input.
- A small set of breadth/depth layers replaces an unmaintainable “thousand
  providers” strategy: KAYAK metasearch, Expedia/Booking deep APIs after
  qualification, Amadeus fast real-price proof, Hotelbeds B2B evaluation,
  Expedia/Booking tracked handoffs, Hostelworld specialist supply and verified
  hotel-direct fallback.
- Live price is a hard product gate. Affiliate deeplinks cannot produce a price
  ranking; one successful live source permits only a named single-source view;
  cross-source comparison needs at least two live sources, and Luvia never
  claims a universal best-market price.
- `booking.live-stay-search.v1` now validates stay, occupancy and destination,
  invokes only readiness-approved Provider adapters and feeds only
  `source=provider_api` plus `live=true` responses into the decision core.
  The authenticated Integration gateway and additive service-only evidence
  schema are prepared for Amadeus and Hotelbeds. Missing credentials, partner
  status, destinations, taxes or response evidence produce `fit_only`, never a
  synthetic rate.
- `booking.affiliate-activation.v1` ranks eleven programmes with six immediate
  application candidates. All remain `not_started`/`paused`; no application,
  contract, credential or provider activation is invented.
- The screenshot-confirmed turquoise, form-heavy Hotel page is legacy UI and
  remains explicitly unaccepted. The binding Hotel slice must reuse the same
  Booking Owner in the bright Demo design with compact search, three decision
  lanes, readable cards, one primary action and full responsive/accessibility
  evidence before a live Hotel provider can be accepted.
- The complete current evidence, ranking, owner application pack and three
  execution waves are binding in

## B1 Booking lifecycle parity now added

- `booking.lifecycle-policy.v1` is the single browserless decision for visible
  manage, modify, cancel, message and status-refresh capabilities.
- An official/partner HTTPS handoff is stored as a normal Booking record before
  the external window is consumed. Repeated clicks within the bounded window
  reuse the record instead of multiplying bookings.
- External completion remains `forwarded`/unconfirmed until provider or message
  evidence returns. A click is never reported as a completed booking.
- A provider chat button exists only for an actual verified email thread.
  Modify/cancel may bootstrap the already existing verified contact-discovery
  path, but arbitrary messages never guess an address.
- Tiqets includes prepared catalogue, availability, create, confirm, get order,
  get ticket and cancel adapter operations. Viator remains constrained to the
  officially authorized partner/merchant operations. Ticketmaster Discovery is
  search/handoff only. None becomes `connected` without partner activation,
  provider-specific Edge transport and live reconciliation evidence.

## `.139` Place-route counterevidence and `.143` integrity correction

Public `.139` operation disproved the generic route assumptions in three
concrete ways: Hotel cards displayed `Eintritt`/`Tickets prüfen`; a nightlife
reservation action could open an unrelated A-ROSA/Straubinger Hotel; and partial
Hotel-name overlap could pass as property identity. A safe HTTPS URL alone is
therefore insufficient.

The accepted `.143` correction first applies a Place-Kind gate: lodging never
inherits ticket/admission actions, and activity/nightlife is not persisted or
routed as restaurant merely because a reservation may exist. It then requires
the resolved URL or verified-email route to match the exact selected venue or
property through provider place ID, normalized name/address or a
provider-specific property/product identifier. Generic corporate pages,
sibling properties, weak name-token overlap and cross-venue targets fail
closed. Route-cache keys include Place and route identity and are invalidated
when those facts change.

The visible local Place/Booking fixture is **4/4 green** and the focused
identity/source/unit tests cover the concrete cross-venue negatives plus exact
OpenTable, TheFork, ticket and Hotel provider/property positives. The Edge
Function `booking-route-resolve` **2.7 is ACTIVE as version 13 with JWT
verification retained**. Runtime commit `11ff01ae`, Worker version
`a8db9b4a-ace5-4144-b86c-db61d9586ccf`, 100% Stable Integration traffic,
197/197 Safe Regression and 24/24 byte-identical public assets complete the
`.143` acceptance. Public Hotel search fails honestly when its Places provider
is unavailable. A compound Tonfink find/check sentence remains explicit new AI
counterevidence and is next; it does not reopen the route-identity acceptance.

## Provider activation is separate work

Tiqets and Viator are the closest transaction-API counterparts to the existing
TheFork/OpenTable-style architecture. Their local adapters are real interfaces,
not mocks, but commercial/API access, credentials, Edge transport, bounded
health diagnostics, idempotency, receipts, reconciliation and public E2E must
all be completed before `connected`. Official booking URLs are useful now;
they remain external handoffs rather than Luvia-created reservations.
